
# Advanced Networks

Welcome to the **Advanced Networks** repository!

This repository is dedicated to exploring advanced networking concepts, protocols, and techniques. Starting with a review of fundamental networking principles, we will progressively dive into more complex topics, including routing protocols, network architectures, and advanced networking mechanisms.

The goal is to combine **theoretical knowledge with practical experience** through course summaries, hands-on exercises, and detailed explanations.

---

## Courses

This section provides structured summaries of the different course chapters, designed to make learning and revision easier.

Each chapter includes:
-  **Course Summaries:** Clear and concise explanations of key concepts.
-  **Q&A:** Questions and answers to reinforce understanding.
-  **Hands-on Exercises:** Practical tasks to apply theoretical knowledge.
-  **Key Takeaways:** Important concepts and points to remember.

---

## Practical Works (TPs)

This section focuses on the practical side of advanced networking.

For each practical assignment (TP), you will find:
-  **Detailed Solutions:** Step-by-step solutions to the proposed exercises.
-  **Interactive Explanations:** Insights into how and why each solution works.
-  **Practical Examples:** Commands, configurations, and demonstrations whenever applicable.
-  **Learning Objectives:** The main networking concepts covered by each practical assignment.
